Coaching

A demanding, supportive outside view to clarify where you're going, decide faster and have more impact. The first session is free.

Book the free session

Who it's for

Executives and founders

One-to-one coaching

You carry decisions alone and want a thinking partner who knows the reality on the ground.

  • Vision and roadmap
  • Hard decisions and priorities
  • Leadership in growth and in crisis

Managers and project leads

One-to-one coaching

You're taking on a new role, a bigger team or a project under pressure.

  • Communication and influence
  • Stakeholder management
  • Emotional intelligence and presence

Students and graduates

Career coaching

You're preparing to enter working life or launch a first business.

  • Career plan and mindset
  • Job search and interviews
  • Starting a business

Teams

In-company workshops, on site or remote

You want to align a leadership team or re-energise a project team.

  • Speak-Up strategy sessions
  • Building a high-performance culture
  • Soft-skills workshops (Softies method)

How coaching works

  1. Free session

    We review your situation and what you want to change.

  2. Goals

    Two or three concrete goals and a session rhythm that suits you.

  3. Sessions

    Discussion, role play and actions to try between sessions.

  4. Review

    What changed, what's left and how to keep going on your own.
