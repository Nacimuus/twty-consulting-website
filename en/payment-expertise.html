How an assessment works

  1. Scoping

    A 30-minute call to understand the project, deadlines and stakes.

  2. Review

    Documentation review and interviews with your teams and partners.

  3. Gaps

    Risks identified across certification, security, schedule and organisation.

  4. Debrief

    Report and prioritised roadmap, presented to your decision-makers.
