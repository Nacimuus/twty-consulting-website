How I teach

No lectures, no endless slides. Participants negotiate, present, decide and sometimes fail, on purpose: that's how people grow.

  • Learn by doing: role play, case studies, simulations
  • Serious games: games that teach and engage
  • Step back: a debrief after every exercise to anchor learning
  • Interactive tools: polls, quizzes, Miro and Trello boards
