Nacim Touenti

Trainer, coach, lecturer. And still an international project manager.

Nacim Touenti is a trainer, coach and university lecturer. He founded Twty Consulting to train teams, coach managers and support startups. His edge: he practises what he teaches. For more than ten years he has run international projects in fintech and electronic payments, across Europe and Africa. Today he leads project management across EMEA for a global player in the sector. He created the Softies method for soft skills and runs Entrepreneur Week workshops with universities and companies. He works from Paris, Zurich and Tangier, in French and English.
