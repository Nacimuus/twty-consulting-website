Nacim Touenti

Electronic payments and project management expert, lecturer and trainer, founder of Twty Consulting.

Nacim Touenti is an electronic payments and project management expert, university lecturer and trainer, and the founder of Twty Consulting. He leads project management across EMEA at a global payment solutions provider, where he drives the launch of payment terminals and software, their EMV certifications (L1, L2, L3), PCI DSS compliance and relationships with international card schemes. With more than ten years of experience in fintech, consulting and international rollouts across Europe and Africa, he also lectures at university and designs training programmes. Through Twty Consulting, based in Paris, Zurich and Tangier, he supports companies, training centres and startups with payments expertise, training and project, product and sales coaching.
